# US startup funding application opportunities

Verified: **2026-08-04**  
Focus: **pre-seed/seed, United States, with extra weight on Austin and the San Francisco Bay Area**

## What this list is — and is not

This is a primary-source application snapshot, not a claim to cover every VC or angel fund. Most venture firms do not publish application deadlines, and many do not offer a public submission path at all. A trustworthy Funding Agent should distinguish:

- **Open with a deadline**: the official source publishes a current cutoff.
- **Rolling**: the official source explicitly says applications are reviewed year-round or on a rolling basis.
- **Public pitch form live**: a form is available, but the fund does not publish a deadline or explicitly promise rolling review.
- **Closed / watch**: the current cohort is closed, a deadline passed, or official pages conflict.

The Wikipedia, Purplepath, and Forbes lists supplied by the user were treated only as discovery leads. Every claim below links to an official program, fund, angel network, university, or city source. YC, Antler, and Startup World Cup are omitted from recommendations because the user has already applied.

## Eight practical applications to prioritize

1. **Berkeley SkyDeck Batch 23 — apply by August 21.** It is the clearest currently open Bay Area accelerator: industry-agnostic, global eligibility, six months in Berkeley, and roughly 20 cohort startups selected for a $210,000 investment. [Official application and timeline](https://skydeck.berkeley.edu/apply/)
2. **Central Texas Angel Network — apply by September 6 at 5:00 PM CT.** This is the strongest dated Austin investor route in this scan and accepts U.S. companies at pre-seed, seed, and Series A; Texas headquarters are not required. [Official 2026 funding cycles](https://www.ctan.com/funding-cycle-information)
3. **Neo Residency — apply now.** Neo's official announcement says startup teams are admitted on a rolling basis for a three-month San Francisco residency and receive a $750,000 uncapped investment, compute credits, and mentorship. [Official program and application](https://neo.com/residency) · [Official launch announcement](https://neo.substack.com/p/neo-residency-a-new-era)
4. **Alchemist Flagship — apply now if enterprise, B2B, or deep tech.** The six-month San Francisco program explicitly accepts applications at any time against rolling application dates. Exact next cutoff is not shown in the page text, so the agent should check again weekly. [Official program](https://www.alchemistaccelerator.com/programs) · [Application portal](https://vault.alchemistaccelerator.com/sign-up?flow=register)
5. **LAUNCH — apply now if the product has early traction.** One form considers a startup for Founder University, the Accelerator, or direct investment. LAUNCH states that the Accelerator invests $125,000 and accepts applications on a rolling basis. [Official funding application](https://www.launch.co/apply) · [Official rolling-status notice](https://calacanis.substack.com/p/founders-friends-and-angels-some)
6. **Forum Ventures — apply now if it is B2B software or B2B AI.** The pitch form is reviewed on a rolling basis; the accelerator advertises a $100,000 pre-seed investment and current Summer 2026 investment themes. [Official rolling pitch form](https://lp.forumvc.com/pitch-us-form) · [Official accelerator](https://www.forumvc.com/accelerator) · [Summer 2026 themes](https://callforfounders.forumvc.com/)
7. **Entrepreneurs First U.S. — apply now if the founder/team is still forming.** EF's San Francisco residencies accept U.S.-based applicants on a rolling basis and can take individuals before a cofounder or fully formed idea. [Official U.S. application](https://www.joinef.com/apply-us-funding-fellowship/) · [Official application hub](https://apply.joinef.com/)
8. **Hustle Fund — submit a direct pre-seed pitch.** The Bay Area fund says it is always actively investing in pre-seed software companies, prefers a live MVP and full-time founders, and exposes a direct pitch form. [Official pitch form](https://hustlefund.typeform.com/to/UGTnIt) · [Official founder FAQ](https://hustlefund.net/faq)

If the startup is impact-oriented, substitute **SWAN Impact Network (September 24)** for a generic rolling application. If it is an immigrant-founded company, **Unshackled Ventures** should move into the top eight.

## Product implication for the Funding Agent

Do not store a single `deadline` field and call it done. At minimum, each opportunity needs:

- `application_status`: `open`, `rolling`, `public_form`, `closed`, `watch`, `conflict`
- `deadline_at` plus timezone, nullable
- `next_verification_at`
- `apply_url` and `official_source_url`
- `last_verified_at`, `confidence`, and a short verification note
- eligibility tags for geography, stage, sector, founder profile, university/age restrictions
- whether acceptance guarantees capital, merely creates investor access, or provides no direct funding

The agent should alert on source changes as well as dates. The 500 Global, South Park Commons, Capital Factory, Moonshots, and SKU examples show why a live form or “Apply” button is not enough to declare a program currently open.
